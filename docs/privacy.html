Memo

Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026

Memo is a flash card app for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: the developer of Memo does not collect, receive or store any of your data. Memo has no servers of its own, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your decks and settings

Your decks, cards, practice progress, bookmarks and settings are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, they are also kept in your own private iCloud storage so that your devices stay the same. That storage belongs to your Apple account; the developer cannot see or access it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

AI-assisted import

This feature is off until you turn it on. If you connect Claude (Anthropic) or ChatGPT (OpenAI) with your own API key and choose a file to import, Memo sends that file and any instructions you add directly from your device to the service you chose, under your own account. Nothing passes through the developer. The service's own terms and privacy policy apply to what you send, and it may charge your account for each request:

  • Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI Privacy Policy

Your API key is stored in your device's Keychain. It stays on that device and is only ever sent to the service it belongs to.

GitHub repository sync

This feature is also off until you use it. If you sign in with GitHub, you approve access on github.com; Memo never sees your password. The access token GitHub issues is stored in iCloud Keychain, which is encrypted end to end, so your other devices can use it. Decks you push are sent directly from your device to the repository you chose, and decks you pull come directly from it. GitHub's Privacy Statement applies to that repository. Practice progress, bookmarks and practice options are never sent.

Files you import and export

When you import a file or share a deck, the file is read or written only where you choose, such as the Files app or a share destination. Memo does not keep copies anywhere else.

Children

Memo does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

  • Delete decks in the app, or delete the app to remove what is stored on the device.
  • Remove the copy in iCloud from Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.
  • Revoke GitHub access at github.com/settings/applications, or sign out in Memo's Sync settings.
  • Revoke an AI API key in the Anthropic or OpenAI console, or disconnect it in Memo's AI Connection settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
