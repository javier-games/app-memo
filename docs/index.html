Memo

Flash cards for iPhone, made for learning that sticks.

  • Decks and cardsA front, a back and an optional hint on each side. Name, icon and colour for every deck.
  • Practice your wayRandom, in order, least practised first, or only the cards you bookmarked. Each deck keeps its own options.
  • Progress that means somethingEvery card counts its correct answers toward a target you set, and you choose what a wrong answer does.
  • On all your devicesDecks and settings stay the same on every device signed in to your iCloud account, and nowhere else.
  • Bring your materialImport JSON or CSV, or turn a text file or PDF into cards with Claude or ChatGPT, using your own API key.
  • Keep decks in GitHubPush and pull decks to a repository of your own when you choose.
